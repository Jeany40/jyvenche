# Client Workflow & Onboarding

A structured approach to high-end project delivery.

## 1. Inquiry & Discovery
- **Submission**: Client completes the "Start Project" form.
- **Review**: Studio triggers an internal review of budget, timeline, and fit.
- **Intro Call**: 15–30 minute discovery call to align on high-level goals.

## 2. Proposal & Agreement
- **Drafting**: Detailed scope of work (SOW) sent via email or Notion.
- **Agreement**: Signing and deposit payment (50% typical).
- **Setup**: Notion Client Portal (`Client Portal — Standard` template) and Slack invitation.

## 3. Project Management
- **Tasks**: All client tasks are tagged as `Client Visible` in Notion.
- **Visibility**: Only linked views filtered for the specific project and `Client Visible = true` are shared.
- **Communication**: Weekly updates provided directly within the portal's "Updates" section.

## 4. Development & Feedback
- **Phased Delivery**: Shared staging URL for iterative review.
- **Sprint Checks**: Weekly or bi-weekly updates on progress.

## 5. Launch & Handoff
- **Verification**: Final QA across mobile/desktop.
- **Training**: Brief walkthrough of CMS or management tools.
- **Handover**: Ownership transfer and final invoice settlement.
